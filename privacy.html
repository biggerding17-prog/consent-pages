Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-17

This policy covers a privately used OAuth application that runs only on its owner's local computer.

Data collected

None. After the owner grants consent, the application accesses files in the owner's own Google account solely to read them and, when the owner asks, to update them. No data is sent anywhere else.

Data sharing

No data is shared with, sold to, or used by any third party.

Storage

OAuth credentials are stored only on the owner's local machine and are never uploaded to any server.

Revoking access

The owner can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
